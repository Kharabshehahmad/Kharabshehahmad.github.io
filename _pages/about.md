I am a PhD Candidate in the Department of Economics at the **University at Albany, SUNY**.

My research focuses on **Health Economics, Public Economics, and Applied Microeconomics**, with a particular interest in physician location, healthcare access, and the effects of healthcare workforce disruptions on population health.

### Education

- **Ph.D. in Econometrics and Quantitative Economics** *(in progress)* — University at Albany, SUNY
- **M.A. in Economics**, 2021 — Texas Tech University
- **M.A. in Business Economics**, 2016 — University of Jordan
- **B.A. in Business Economics**, 2014 — University of Jordan

### Current Work

I am working on three projects examining the geographic and institutional determinants of healthcare access:

- **Residency program closures** and downstream effects on mortality *(with Hyoshin Ki)* — selected for presentation at the **ASSA 2027 Annual Meeting** in Washington, D.C.
- **Neighborhood deprivation** and geographic access to physicians *(with Chun-Yu Ho)*
- **School quality and physician location** in New York State *(with Hyoshin Ki and Chun-Yu Ho)*

See my [Research](/publications/) page for more details.

### Teaching

I have taught **Public Finance** (AECO 355) and **Intermediate Microeconomics** (AECO 300) at the University at Albany, with teaching evaluations consistently above the departmental average. I received the **Helen Horowitz Award for Outstanding Teaching by a Graduate Student** in 2025.

See my [Teaching](/teaching/) page for evaluations and course history.
