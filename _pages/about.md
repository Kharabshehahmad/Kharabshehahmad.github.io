Welcome! I am a PhD Candidate in the Department of Economics at the **University at Albany, SUNY**, on the **2026–2027 academic job market**.

My research is in **health economics**, with a focus on physician location, healthcare access, and the effects of healthcare workforce disruptions on population health. My job market paper, *"When Training Stops: The Effects of Residency Program Closures on Mortality Rate"* (with Hyoshin Ki), examines how the closure of medical residency programs affects local population mortality.

I will present this work at the **2027 ASSA Annual Meeting in Washington, D.C., as part of a session nominated by the American Society of Health Economists (ASHEcon), and at the 2026 CAPS Annual Conference at Syracuse University.

Beyond research, I am an instructor who has taught Public Finance, Intermediate Microeconomics, and Principles of Macro/Microeconomics at UAlbany, with teaching evaluations consistently above the departmental average. In 2025, I received the Helen Horowitz Award for Outstanding Teaching by a Graduate Student.

For more, see my [Job Market Paper](/files/kharabsheh_jmp.pdf), [Research](/publications/), [Teaching](/teaching/), and [CV](/files/kharabsheh_cv.pdf).
